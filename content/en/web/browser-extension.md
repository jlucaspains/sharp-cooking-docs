The Sharp Cooking Recipe Importer is a browser extension that lets you import recipes from any webpage directly into Sharp Cooking — no copy-pasting required.

![Chrome Extension](https://blog.lpains.net/images/posts/sharp-cooking-extension.png)

## Installing the extension

The extension is available for **Google Chrome** and Chromium-based browsers (Edge, Brave, etc.). You can find it in the [Chrome Web Store](https://chrome.google.com/webstore) by searching for "Sharp Cooking Recipe Importer", or install it manually from the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/jlucaspains/sharp-cooking-web/tree/main/browser-extensions).

## Importing a recipe

1. Navigate to any recipe webpage in your browser.
2. Click the **Sharp Cooking Recipe Importer** extension icon in your browser toolbar.
3. The extension parses the recipe on the page and displays a **QR code**.
4. Open Sharp Cooking on your phone or tablet and use the **Add from share code** option.
5. Scan the QR code — the recipe will be imported into your app automatically.

{{< hint type=tip >}}
The same recipe websites supported by the in-app URL import are supported by the extension. If a website doesn't work, report it in the [GitHub issues](https://github.com/jlucaspains/sharp-cooking-web/issues).
{{< /hint >}}
