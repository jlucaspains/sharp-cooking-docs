Recipe categories let you group your recipes for easier browsing and filtering. You can assign one or more categories to each recipe when creating or editing it.

## Assigning categories to a recipe

When creating or editing a recipe, scroll to the **Category** field and type the category name. You can add multiple categories to the same recipe.

## Filtering by category

On the recipe list page, categories appear as filter buttons at the top. Tap a category to show only the recipes belonging to that group. Tap **All** to remove the filter and show every recipe.

## "All" category position

By default, the **All** category appears first in the list. You can change this in the **Options** page — toggle the **All category first** option off to move it to the end of the category list.

{{< hint type=tip >}}
Categories are a great way to separate breakfast, lunch, dinner, and dessert recipes so you can quickly find what you need.
{{< /hint >}}
